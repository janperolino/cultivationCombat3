### Runtime and recovery rules

`src/encounter.ts` owns waiting → active → completed/defeated, plus returning → active. Lifecycle is opt-in through the host's skirmish construction; original solo and stationary sentinel scenes remain unchanged. `src/content.ts` validates/freeze-copies leash, disengagement, bounded return timeout, revive distance and duration. Only seats that have joined are enrolled. Unused seats do not receive enemy damage or prevent solo defeat. Enrolled participants remain enrolled and vulnerable through disconnect/Leave for the lifetime of this host session. Joining or recovering never heals or restarts anything; joining an already terminal encounter requires explicit Retry.

All authored enemies defeated ends the attempt in victory. All enrolled players defeated ends it in defeat; simultaneous elimination resolves as defeat. Terminal transitions cancel all projectiles, casts, warnings, revive channels, effects and momentum. Combat/controls remain paused until Retry. Either authenticated player may retry a terminal attempt, restoring the construction-time player/enemy profiles at terrain-safe spawn heights. Terrain cells, revisions, scars, shared clock and connection credentials are preserved. No reward, currency, experience, death penalty, save or campaign checkpoint was added.

Hold R within **4m in 3D for 2 seconds** while stationary to revive an enrolled fallen ally. Releasing R, movement/climb intent or residual velocity, cast/dodge attempts, leaving range, target loss or damage interrupts progress. Revival restores **50% maximum health, 50 qi, zero ward**, clears cooldown/dodge/movement state, and grants no invulnerability. Regeneration resumes on subsequent ticks. Revive is fresh held input under the existing 250ms stale-input cutoff, not a replayable cast action; disconnect stops the channel. Progress and completion are host-owned.

Leash is **120m in 3D from an enemy's authored anchor**. Three seconds with no living participant inside any anchor's leash also disengages the group. Returning cancels all combat and pauses player controls while living enemies fly home. Arrival or an **8-second fallback** restores the entire enemy roster, including previously defeated enemies, and begins a new attempt. Only enemies regain resources; players and terrain retain their state. If enemies were already home, returning/reset may finish in one tick. Fallback restoration is deliberate bounded teleport recovery, not proof that every route can be navigated.

### Input and replication boundaries

Protocol **5** adds optional encounter ID, input epoch, attempt number, phase, enrolled player IDs and bounded revive progress to frames. Strict validation rejects malformed phases, nonfinite/out-of-range progress, invalid participants/targets, regressing epochs/attempts and omitted lifecycle data after it was established. Frame/terrain commits remain atomic and 64KiB/1024-cell limits remain intact. Hosts and clients from earlier protocols must restart/reload.

Encounter commands/actions carry the current epoch; late previous-epoch packets cannot execute after a reset. Authority transitions clear motion intent and expire pending discrete actions while retaining bounded monotonic acknowledgments. Retry uses the caller's authenticated generation and terminal epoch through `/api/retry`; duplicate or reordered requests cannot reset an active/later attempt. The simulation also discards the input batch on the exact tick an internal return completes, preventing a queued attack from crossing that boundary. Client outboxes reset to the acknowledged sequence and motion prediction/interpolation histories reset on epoch changes. Prediction respects paused lifecycle states. Reconnect still grants no healing/revival or action replay.
