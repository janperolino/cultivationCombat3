## Deliverable and boundaries

- Define stable IDs and explicit typed definitions for the three existing techniques, the current stationary sentinel/enemy baseline, and one bounded encounter composition. Keep definitions separate from mutable runtime state.
- Validate numeric ranges/finite values, unique IDs, supported behaviors, cross-references and bounded counts at the content entry point. Reject malformed content with useful errors before mutating a live world.
- Route existing technique configuration and the baseline enemy/encounter setup through definitions where practical; avoid an unused schema layer or rewriting the whole simulation. Preserve existing tuning unless a documented correctness issue requires change.
- Maintain host-owned gameplay configuration. Clients cannot select arbitrary definitions or override damage, costs, teams or spawn counts. Preserve reliable actions, replication bounds and recovery behavior.
- Keep enemy behavior stationary in this phase; no multi-enemy AI, boss patterns, revive/death policy, persistent rewards, campaign content, new services or production art. Do not settle open cultivation paths or campaign ownership/death decisions by inventing defaults.
- Document the extension points and concrete remaining fixed-player/rival/replication assumptions for M2.P2. Dynamic enemy roster replication may be deferred explicitly; do not claim it works based on data definitions alone.

Definitions are copied and deeply frozen separately from mutable simulation actors.
